OUR STORY

More than coffee. It's a feeling.

Velvet Bean started with one small coffee machine, a passion for roasting and a dream to create a place where everyone feels welcome.

Today, we roast carefully selected beans and serve them alongside fresh pastries, desserts and café favorites.
